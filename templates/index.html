Printer help

Recalibrate after changing label size

Do this at the printer itself.

  1. Load the new labels and close the cover.
  2. Switch the printer off for 3 seconds.
  3. Hold the feed button on top and switch it back on, keeping the button held.
  4. Release when the printer beeps or the feed light blinks. It will then calibrate against the new stock.

Feed button light

  • Solid blue — ready. Press to feed a label.
  • Solid purple — calibrating.
  • Solid red — press the cover down to close it.
  • Blinking red — restart the printer, then recalibrate.
